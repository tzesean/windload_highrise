Notes

  1. The calculation shall only be used for:
    1. a. lateral forces acting on rectangular enclosed structures only
    2. b. structures with natural frequency > 0.2 Hz
    3. building less than 200 m high
    4. structures with roof spans less than 100 m
    5. structures other than off-shore structures, bridges and transmission towers
  2. In this calculation, Cpe value = 0.8. Leeward External Pressure Coefficient is calculated automatically.
  3. Internal pressure coefficient, sidewall pressure coefficient and underside roof pressure ares not considered in this calculation.
  4. As stated in MS1553, different height from user input is used to calculate windward pressure, while the highest level is used for calculating leeward pressure.
  5. For Structures with natural frequency > 1.0 Hz, Cdyn is taken as 1.0.
  6. For dynamic response factor in this calculation, s = 0 for SLS, and s = z for ULS.
  7. Peak along-wind acceleration is calculated.
  8. Frictional Drag (Section 5.5) is not considered in this calculation.
  9. Total drift and inter-storey drift shall be < h/500.
  10. Floor acceleration shall be < 0.01g for residential buildings and < 0.015g for other structures as stated in the code.
